# Business Insights

Use the query results to document observations in this format:

1. **Customer Base:** Report customer count, customer segments and multi-account behavior.
2. **Transactions:** Identify the highest-value transaction types and channels.
3. **Trends:** Identify strongest and weakest months and calculate MoM growth.
4. **Accounts:** Compare account types and total active balances.
5. **Loans:** Identify loan types with the highest exposure and overdue exposure.
6. **Branches:** Identify branches with the strongest transaction and loan performance.
7. **Customer Risk:** Highlight customers with overdue loans and active accounts that appear dormant.
8. **Recommendations:** Suggest actions around digital-channel adoption, customer retention, dormant-account engagement, loan-risk monitoring and branch resource allocation.
